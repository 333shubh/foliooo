05

The cartoon look

Three.js, drawn like a cel. Three stacked rules make the look, and every world and prop follows them.

1 · Three tones

Light hits a surface in exactly three hard bands: shade, base, light. Rocks and crystals are faceted; hills, pods and clouds are smooth.

2 · Hairline ink

A post pass draws 1px lines wherever depth, crease or object changes. Each line is a darker shade of the surface under it, and lines fade into the fog with distance.

3 · Grain on top

One animated noise layer sits over the canvas and the UI together, so paper and paint feel like the same print.
